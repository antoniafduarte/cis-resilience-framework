# Subsystem E — On-Farm Performance

This folder contains the Excel tool for assessing on-farm irrigation resilience and performance.

## Main workbook

`RI_SE_on_farm_performance_tool_v1_1_public.xlsx`

## Assessment focus

Subsystem E evaluates farm-level conditions that influence the capacity of the irrigation system to convert delivered water into effective crop service.

## Typical inputs

- Irrigated area and crop distribution
- Irrigation technologies and application efficiencies
- Crop calendars and water-demand information
- Soil-water and irrigation-management data
- Equipped and effectively irrigated areas
- Assessment period and data-source information

## Main outputs

- Irrigation-technology and application-performance indicators
- Water-demand and soil-water indicators
- Irrigated-area and service-capacity indicators
- Normalised indicator and dimension scores
- Subsystem E resilience score and dashboard

## Workflow

1. Define crop groups, irrigated area and irrigation technologies.
2. Enter crop, soil, water-demand and management data.
3. Verify units, area totals and technology shares.
4. Review calculated indicators and validation checks.
5. Interpret changes together with crop-pattern and infrastructure evolution.

## Notes

Some indicators may co-trend because of system expansion or changes in irrigation technology. Local crop calendars, efficiencies and service assumptions must be documented and updated when the assessment is transferred to another system.
