# Subsystem D — Energy Performance

This folder contains the Excel tool for assessing the energy performance of a pressurised collective irrigation system.

## Main workbook

`RI_SD_energy_performance_tool_v1_1_public.xlsx`

## Assessment focus

Subsystem D evaluates the relationship between water service, pumping requirements, energy consumption and operating performance.

## Typical inputs

- Abstracted, supplied and delivered water volumes
- Pumping hours and installed capacity
- Energy consumption and energy costs
- Pumping head or pressure requirements
- Energy tariffs and assessment period
- Source and confidence information

## Main outputs

- Energy-intensity indicators
- Volume-related energy-performance indicators
- Pumping and operating-performance indicators
- Normalised indicator and dimension scores
- Subsystem D resilience score and dashboard

## Workflow

1. Define the assessment period and energy-accounting boundaries.
2. Enter water-volume, pumping and energy data.
3. Verify units, tariffs and system boundaries.
4. Review calculated indicators and validation checks.
5. Compare energy performance with service delivery and hydraulic conditions.

## Notes

Indicators based on abstracted and supplied volumes may be closely related. Their interpretation should consider conveyance losses, pumping configuration, tariffs and changes in the service area.
