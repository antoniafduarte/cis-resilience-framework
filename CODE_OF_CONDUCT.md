# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive and constructive environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- welcome questions and constructive criticism;
- focus discussions on evidence, methods and reproducibility;
- acknowledge contributions appropriately;
- protect confidential, sensitive and personal information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination and personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, plagiarism and misrepresentation of scientific results.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that do not comply with these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository maintainer through the contact options associated with the GitHub account.
